## For this lab we used a two bit adder instead of two one bit adders. 

### Summary
In this lab we learned how to implement adders using our established knowledge of verilog code. We also explored binary math with carry out and carry in bits.

### 1 - How might you add more than two bits together?
To add more than two bits together you need more inputs, carry ins, and outputs to express the results on the board.

### 2 - What is the importance of the XOR gate in an adder?
The XOR gate gets the result of the adder and we can use an and gate to catch and use any carry outs/ins later. 

### 3 - What is the largest number a two bit adder can handle? What happens when you go over?
The largest number a two bit adder can handle is 6 or 11 + 11, if you go over you need more bits to properly express it.
